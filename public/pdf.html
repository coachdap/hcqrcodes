PDF tools

Merge waivers and packets, pull out pages, shrink big scans for email, and turn pages into images (or photos into a PDF).
    Merge

    Files are joined top to bottom. Drag rows or use the arrows to change the order. Images become one page each.

      Split & extract
      Commas separate pieces. 1-3 is pages 1 to 3, 5 is just page 5, 8- is page 8 to the end.
      Off: each piece becomes its own PDF (downloaded as a ZIP).
        Compress
          PDF → images
            Images → PDF

            One image per page, in list order. Handy for turning phone photos of forms into a single PDF.
